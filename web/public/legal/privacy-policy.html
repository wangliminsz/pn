Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how proNotify collects, uses, shares, stores and protects personal data when you visit our website, create an account or use our property and rent-reminder service. We process personal data in accordance with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) ("PDPA") and other applicable laws.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to personal data we process as a data controller when you visit pronotify.site, register for an account, purchase a plan, contact support or use the proNotify workspace.

Where your agency uploads tenant, landlord or group-member data into proNotify, your agency is generally the data controller for that data and proNotify acts as a data processor. In that case, your agency is responsible for providing notices and obtaining any required consent from those individuals.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account and business data

  • Name, email address, phone number, company name, login credentials and account settings.
  • Billing and subscription details, including plan, payment status, payment date, amount, currency and Omise charge references. We do not store full card numbers or PromptPay credentials.
  • Support communications and any information you choose to provide to us.

2.2 Property and lease data

  • Room or property names, rent amounts, payment cycles, reminder days, lease start and end dates, owner or responsible staff, notes and LINE group IDs.
  • Documents, images and files uploaded to the workspace or stored from LINE groups.
  • Google Calendar connection details and event identifiers where you enable calendar sync.

2.3 Technical and usage data

  • IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, language preference, pages visited, timestamps and referring URLs.
  • Security logs, login attempts, error logs and audit records.
  • Cookies, local storage and similar technologies used to keep you signed in and remember preferences.

2.4 Data from third parties

We may receive data from LINE (such as group IDs and message events), Google (calendar identifiers and event status), Omise (payment status and transaction references) and our email or hosting providers.

3. How we use data

  • Create and manage your account, workspace and user permissions.
  • Provide the service, including automated rent reminders, lease-expiry reminders, group document storage and calendar sync.
  • Process payments, confirm subscriptions and manage renewals or refunds.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate service, billing or security notices.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, troubleshoot errors and monitor performance.
  • Improve our product, features and user experience using aggregated or de-identified information.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your property, lease or group data for advertising.

4. Legal bases

We rely on one or more of the following legal bases under the PDPA:

  • Contract: to provide the service you have purchased and to manage your account.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure and improve the service, prevent fraud and communicate essential service information.
  • Consent: where we ask for your consent, for example for optional communications or certain cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: to keep records or disclose information where required by law.
  • Vital interests or public interest: in limited circumstances permitted by law.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We may share personal data with:

  • Service providers that help us run proNotify, including cloud hosting, storage, email delivery, analytics, security and customer support providers.
  • Payment providers such as Omise, to process PromptPay payments and refunds.
  • Integration providers such as LINE and Google, when you connect those services and enable messaging or calendar sync.
  • Professional advisers and authorities where necessary to comply with law, enforce our rights or protect users.
  • Successors in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

We require service providers to protect personal data and use it only for the purposes we specify. We do not sell personal data or share it for third-party advertising.

6. International transfers

Our service providers may store or process data outside Thailand, including in Singapore and other countries where they operate. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we take steps required by the PDPA, such as entering into appropriate agreements or relying on permitted exceptions, to protect the data.

7. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including:

  • Account and workspace data: while your account is active, and for up to 12 months after closure for backup, dispute and recovery purposes.
  • Payment and tax records: for at least 5 years or as required by Thai law.
  • Security and access logs: generally for up to 90 days, unless needed longer for an investigation.
  • Support communications: for up to 24 months after the request is resolved.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it. Backup copies may persist for a limited period before being overwritten.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls, tenant isolation, encrypted connections, password hashing, audit logs and restricted staff access. No system is completely secure. Please use a strong password and keep your login details confidential.

9. Your rights

Subject to the PDPA and applicable exceptions, you have the right to:

  • Access and obtain a copy of your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete or destroy data that is no longer necessary or unlawfully processed.
  • Restrict the processing of your data.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Receive your data in a machine-readable format and request transfer where technically feasible.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Committee of Thailand.

To exercise your rights, email antglobalproperty3@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity. We will respond within the period required by law. If your data was uploaded by your agency, please contact your agency first; we will assist them as a processor.

10. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser local storage to keep you signed in, remember your language and theme preferences, protect against fraud and understand basic usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may prevent the service from working properly.

11. Children

proNotify is a business service intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a change materially affects how we use personal data, we will provide reasonable notice before it takes effect.
