Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how cancellation and refunds work for the proNotify Annual Plan. It forms part of our Terms of Service. Please read it before purchasing a plan.

Plan summary: proNotify Annual Plan — ฿2,000 THB for 365 days. One-time payment via PromptPay QR through Omise. No auto-renewal.

1. Overview

Because proNotify is a digital service that provides access immediately after payment, fees are generally non-refundable once your subscription is activated. However, we review genuine billing errors, duplicate charges and cases where the service was not provided. Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under applicable consumer protection law.

2. Cancellation

You may cancel future renewal at any time. Since proNotify does not auto-renew, cancellation means you will not purchase another 365-day term. To record your cancellation, email support@pronotify.site from your registered email address.

  • Notice period: please send your cancellation request at least 7 days before the end of your current 365-day term.
  • Effect: your workspace remains available until the end of the paid term. No further charges will be made because auto-renewal is not enabled.
  • Cancellation fee: none.
  • Refund on cancellation: cancellation does not automatically create a refund for the current term. Refunds are considered under section 3 below.

3. When refunds may be approved

We may approve a refund, in whole or in part, if:

  • You were charged twice for the same plan or the same billing period.
  • You were charged an incorrect amount or were charged after a cancellation that we had confirmed.
  • You paid but we failed to activate your workspace and could not resolve the issue within a reasonable time.
  • The payment was unauthorised or fraudulent, and this is confirmed by our payment provider.
  • Applicable law requires a refund.

Refund requests should be made within 7 days of the payment date, except where a longer period is required by law. We may ask for information to verify the payment and the issue.

4. Non-refundable cases

Subject to applicable law, we generally do not refund:

  • Fees for a term that has already started, where the service was made available and worked as described.
  • Requests based on a change of mind after activation.
  • Requests caused by incorrect data entered by you, including wrong LINE group IDs, reminder days or lease dates.
  • Requests caused by third-party outages or restrictions, such as LINE or Google service limitations, unless the issue is widespread and prolonged and we cannot provide a reasonable workaround.
  • Add-on or custom work that has already been performed.
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for breach of our Terms of Service or unlawful use.
  • Payment-processing fees, where permitted by law.

5. How to request a refund

To request a refund, email support@pronotify.site with the subject "Refund request" and include:

  1. Your registered account email and company name.
  2. Your tenant code (if available).
  3. The payment date, amount and currency (THB).
  4. The Omise charge ID or the reference shown on your payment receipt, if available.
  5. A description of the issue and any supporting evidence (for example a screenshot or receipt).

We will acknowledge your request and may ask for additional information. We aim to make a decision within 7 business days after receiving the information we need. If we approve the refund, we will confirm the amount and the original payment method used.

6. Refund method

Approved refunds are returned to the same payment method used for the original payment. For PromptPay payments processed by Omise, the refund is sent back through Omise to the original payment channel. We do not issue refunds to a different card, bank account or third party.

If the original payment method is no longer available, we will work with Omise to identify a lawful alternative. We may request identity verification before processing a refund.

7. Processing time

Once approved, refunds are typically processed within 7–14 business days. The time it takes for the funds to appear in your account may depend on Omise, your bank or your payment provider. We will provide a confirmation when the refund has been submitted.

8. Auto-renewal and renewal

proNotify does not use automatic renewal. We do not store your payment details and will not charge you automatically. Your plan ends after 365 days. If you want to continue, you must manually purchase another term. Because there is no auto-renewal, there is no automatic charge to cancel.

If you choose to renew and later request a refund for the new term, this policy applies to that new payment. Please review the plan and price on the checkout page before paying.

9. Chargebacks

Please contact us before starting a chargeback so we can try to resolve the issue quickly. If a chargeback is raised, we may suspend the account while the payment provider investigates. We may provide transaction information to Omise and the relevant financial institution as required to respond to the dispute.
