Sales and technical support

For questions about the product, onboarding, LINE groups, Google Calendar sync or your workspace, email support@pronotify.site. Please include your account email, company name and tenant code (if available) so we can help faster.

We aim to respond within one business day. Support is available Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 ICT, excluding public holidays in Thailand.

Billing, cancellation and refunds

For billing questions, cancellation requests or refunds, email support@pronotify.site with the subject "Billing" or "Refund request". Include your payment date, amount, Omise charge ID and a description of the issue.

Our plan is a one-time payment of ฿3,000 THB for 365 days and does not auto-renew. Please send cancellation requests at least 7 days before the end of your current term. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method via Omise.

Privacy requests

To access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, or to withdraw consent, email support@pronotify.site with the subject "Privacy request". We may need to verify your identity before acting on the request.
